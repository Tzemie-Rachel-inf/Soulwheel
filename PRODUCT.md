# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A single player at their own computer, rolling fantasy heroes for fun: spinning wheels to build a soul, then sending that one hero down the road on adventures until their story ends.

## Product Purpose

Soulwheel is a wheel-spinning hero generator and life sim. Every attribute of a hero (ancestry, stats, weapon, armour, traits) is rolled on a weighted wheel; the hero then lives one life in a persistent world of houses, a Demon Lord, a graveyard and a Hall of Fame. Success is that every spin feels like it matters.

## Positioning

One hero at a time, start to finish, in a world that remembers them. Odds are always shown; the wheels never lie about their weights.

## Operating Context

Played in a desktop browser, in sessions of a few minutes to an hour. Tabs: Create (roll a soul), Adventures (the hero's road), Chronicle (the world's record), and a dev-only Wheels inspector.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 19 + Vite + Tailwind 4; motion via framer-motion; icons from lucide-react.
- Wheels are painted on canvas (src/ui/wheel/paintWheel.ts).
- Git repo in chronicle-react (baseline commit 2026-10-13). Older folder backups live in E:\Chronicle\backups\ (pre-fantasy-redesign holds the cathedral-glass look).
- Sound effects with a mute toggle; prefers-reduced-motion is honoured.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Soulwheel. Tagline: "Roll a soul. Send it into the world."
- High fantasy, not modern UI: the user asked for more gold, more banners, a royal court in heraldry (confirmed 2026-10-13).

## Evidence on Hand

All content is generated by the game's own config (src/config). No testimonials, press, or external assets.

## Product Principles

1. Every spin matters: the wheel and its result are always the centre of attention.
2. Show the odds honestly.
3. One life at a time; the world persists.
4. The fantasy dresses the game; it never hides a control or a number.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable tabs (ARIA tabs pattern), visible focus, reduced-motion support, text contrast at 4.5:1 or better.
