# Soulwheel app shell

Scope: the whole app (header, tabs, panels, buttons, wheel rim, reveal, chronicle). Mode: Operate.
Audience: one player rolling and playing a single hero. Constraint: no control, number or odds may be hidden by ornament.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A king's great hall at night. The app is the hall; the tabs are heraldic banners hanging from a gilt rod, and every panel is a gilt-framed board. Refuses the flat dark-dashboard default (rounded grey cards, pill tabs, one accent).

OWN-WORLD: Oxblood velvet ground with a faint damask; ebony panels framed in leaf gold with corner fleurons; banners in azure with gules for the active one, gold fringe and swallowtail ends. Gold rim on the wheel with a gules velvet hub. Type: IM Fell English SC for titles, UnifrakturMaguntia for the Soulwheel wordmark only, Alegreya for body, Alegreya SC for labels.

STORY: The player sees which banner they stand under, spins, reads the result on a gilt-framed board, and enters the world.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header: blackletter wordmark at left; at right, four hanging swallowtail banners on a gilt rod (the tabs), active one longer and crimson. Left column: wheel title in Fell small caps over a gold-rimmed wheel. Right column: the hero's gilt-framed card.

FORM: pinned by the user (royal court & heraldry, option 1 of 4 offered); signature move: tabs as hanging banners. seed: none (concept-seed not run; the user pinned the world from four offered options)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
