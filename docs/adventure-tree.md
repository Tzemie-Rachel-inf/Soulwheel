# Soulwheel: adventure tree



Every wheel a living hero can spin on an adventure, starting at **What Will You Do?**. 147 wheels. The road ends when an option leads nowhere; an option that sets a status or a new role ends the hero's story. Power wheels shared with creation live in [creation-tree.md](creation-tree.md); training ladders in [stat-ladders.md](stat-ladders.md).



## How to read this

- **Weight** is the option's base weight; **Base chance** is its share of the wheel before anything about the character or world changes it (locked options still count here). Options marked *special* only appear for some characters.
- What bends the odds, per character: **Luck** (×(1 + k × luck)), **Stats** (×(1 + k × (stat − 10))), **Fame** (×(1 + k × fame tier)), the character's **race** and **archetype**, and on fights the **chance to win** (your CP ÷ (your CP + foe power)). **Threat** (the world dial) changes odds but not slice sizes.
- **Locked** options show on the wheel but cannot be rolled. A **route** sends the character to one of several wheels depending on who they are.
- Stats floor at 0 at creation; on an adventure, a stat reaching 0 kills the hero.



## Fame



Every adventure roll earns fame: a fight won earns foe power ÷ 25 (×2 for glory, ×1.5 for a trait-only win), a lost fight nothing, an outcome that ends the road earns by rarity (common 2, uncommon 4, rare 8, epic 12, legendary 20, mythical 30; half on training ladders), and options with their own fame use that. Wheels along the way earn nothing.



| Tier | Fame | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Unknown | 0+ | Nobody has heard of them. Yet. |
| Known | 6+ | A name in a few taverns. |
| Renowned | 15+ | Bards want the details. Destiny starts to call. |
| Famous | 32+ | Kingdoms know the name, and the greatest challenges come looking. |
| Legendary | 60+ | Songs in every tongue. Every road they take is a dangerous one. |
| Mythic | 100+ | More story than person now. |
